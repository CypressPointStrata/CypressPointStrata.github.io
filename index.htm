About Cypress Point

Cypress Point Strata is a residential community located at 7511 Minoru Boulevard in Richmond, BC. We are committed to maintaining our building and providing excellent service to our residents.

Strata Council

The Strata Council meets regularly to discuss matters affecting our community. Meeting minutes are published and available for our residents to review.

Community Resources

We maintain comprehensive documentation including bylaws, procedures, and manuals. Access these resources through the links in our navigation menu.
