Community Swimming Pool

Cypress Point Strata NW2050

Cypress Point Strata is proud to offer residents access to a beautiful swimming pool as part of our community amenities.

Pool Facilities

Our outdoor swimming pool provides a refreshing retreat for residents during the warm months. The pool is maintained to the highest standards for safety and cleanliness.

Season and Hours

The pool operates seasonally, typically from late spring through early fall. Specific opening and closing dates, as well as operating hours, are posted in the lobby and on the community bulletin board.

Safety Rules and Regulations

All residents and guests using the pool must follow comprehensive safety guidelines to protect everyone's well-being. Children must be supervised at all times, and swimming at hours outside posted times is not permitted.

Maintenance

Regular maintenance and chemical testing ensure the pool meets all health and safety standards. The pool is closed periodically for cleaning and servicing.

Access and Guest Policy

Access to the pool is restricted to residents and their authorized guests. Guest access policies are outlined in the community bylaws. Questions about pool access or rules can be directed to Administrator Audrey Montero at 604-279-1554.

Amenities and Activities

The pool is a great place to relax, get exercise, and socialize with neighbors. The Strata Council periodically organizes pool-related events and activities for residents.
