Frequently Asked Questions

Cypress Point Strata NW2050

Q: What are strata fees?

Strata fees (also called assessments) are monthly charges paid by all residents to cover the costs of maintaining common property, utilities, insurance, and building operations. These fees are determined by the Strata Council based on annual budgets.

Q: How is the Strata Council elected?

The Strata Council is elected by residents at the Annual General Meeting. All residents are eligible to serve and can vote on Council positions.

Q: How do I request maintenance for common property?

Contact the Strata Administrator, Audrey Montero, by phone at 604-279-1554 or email at cypresspointnw2050@gmail.com to submit a maintenance request.

Q: What do I need to do before renovating my unit?

Refer to the Procedures page for detailed information on the renovation approval process. Most renovations affecting common areas or structural elements require prior approval from the Strata Council.

Q: When is the Annual General Meeting?

The Annual General Meeting is typically held in spring. Specific dates and agendas are posted in the building lobbies in advance.

Q: Can I rent out my unit?

Rental policies are outlined in the Bylaws. Review the Bylaws section for specific provisions regarding rental of units.

Q: What are pool hours and usage rules?

Visit the Pool page for information about hours, safety rules, and guest policies.

Q: How do I get a copy of the Bylaws?

Bylaws are available on this website or by contacting the Administrator at 604-279-1554.

Q: Who do I contact about urgent issues?

For urgent maintenance issues, contact the Administrator immediately at 604-279-1554. For emergencies, call 911.
