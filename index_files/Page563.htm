Resident Resources

Cypress Point Strata NW2050

We provide residents with a variety of resources to help them get the most from their living experience in our community.

Important Documents

Access to bylaws, meeting minutes, financial reports, and other important strata documents is available through this website or by contacting our administrator.

Meeting Schedule

The Annual General Meeting is typically held in the spring. Special general meetings may be called as needed. Notices and agendas are posted in advance.

Community Amenities

Residents enjoy access to shared amenities including the swimming pool and other common facilities. See the Bylaws for details on facility usage, hours, and policies.

Maintenance Requests

Residents can submit maintenance requests for common property issues through the administrator. Emergency repairs are prioritized and handled promptly.

Need Help?

If you have questions about strata issues, need to report a problem, or want to learn more about community procedures, please contact our administrator at 604-279-1554 or cypresspointnw2050@gmail.com.
