Swimming Pool

Welcome to the Cypress Point Strata Swimming Pool! Our pool is a valuable community amenity for residents to enjoy. Please review all rules, regulations, and safety guidelines to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all pool users.

Pool Rules & Regulations

All residents and guests must adhere to the established pool rules and regulations. These guidelines ensure the safety and enjoyment of all community members. Please review the complete rules document for detailed information about hours of operation, capacity, and user responsibilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the pool hours?

Please refer to the Pool Rules and Regulations document for current operating hours. Hours may vary by season and may be subject to change.

Can I bring guests to the pool?

Guests are permitted to use the pool facilities. Please review the guest policy in the Pool Rules and Regulations for specific requirements.

Are there age restrictions?

Children may use the pool, but supervision requirements apply. See the complete rules document for details on age-specific guidelines and supervision responsibilities.

What should I do if the pool is closed?

The pool may be temporarily closed for maintenance or due to water quality issues. Check the maintenance schedule and any posted notices for closure dates.
